# Residence interior kit

Create the 256×256 `bld_m_house.interior` ground-layer kit. It is an occupied old-town home:
walkable timber floor, north-wall trim, fixed table/hearth and storage/bed detail. Keep the south
threshold and resident interaction floor clear and legible as a route. Do not depict the resident,
player, dialogue UI, text, labels, or any baked light halo.
