# CodeCity pixel-art style

Create a small map-scale pixel-art game asset, not concept art or a large illustration.

- Keep the silhouette readable at logical size.
- Use a controlled palette and two or three shading steps.
- Preserve hard pixel edges and nearest-neighbor scaling.
- Do not add labels, captions, UI frames, watermarks, or explanatory text.
- Follow the asset definition's output contract and approved references.
- A generated image is only a pending candidate; visual acceptance is human-only.
