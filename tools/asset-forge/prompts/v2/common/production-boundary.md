# Fable5 production and evidence boundary

Generate only the asset named by the definition and its explicitly declared frames or paired
layers. Keep generous clear padding around the subject and make every required edge fully visible.
Do not hide geometry behind atmospheric effects, props, text, floor planes, or decorative scenery.

The source may be larger than the logical output, but it must support nearest-neighbor reduction
to the exact declared dimensions. Do not enlarge a smaller source. Required transparent outputs
use a perfectly flat removable chroma-key background selected by the job; the key color must not
appear in the subject. No cast shadow may touch the chroma background beyond the declared contact
shadow inside the asset bounds.

Generation establishes only candidate bytes. Record the exact prompt, input-reference hashes,
source hash, extraction/resize recipe, and observed image dimensions. Visual suitability, rights,
runtime integration, and approval remain unknown until their separate gates complete.
