# Fable5 world visual contract

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production pixel-art game asset for CodeCity Inspector

Match the approved `world_visual_master` as one continuous world, without copying its scene
composition. Use a single three-quarter overhead projection, camera elevation about 55 degrees,
with the ground plane visible behind vertical faces. The light comes from the upper-left/back at
twilight. Exterior ambient colors are charcoal, slate blue, desaturated moss green, weathered
brown, and cold stone; windows, lanterns, and hearths use restrained amber accents.

The asset must remain readable at its declared native logical size. Use crisp deliberate pixel
clusters, one-pixel dark outer contours where the silhouette needs separation, two or three
material shading steps, and a small contact shadow for grounded solid objects. Wood, stone, metal,
cloth, foliage, snow, and water must retain distinct textures. Avoid smooth painting, soft focus,
photorealism, vector-flat shapes, mixed projection, or a side-view facade.

Do not add labels, captions, logos, UI frames, watermarks, presentation borders, checkerboards,
asset names, arbitrary characters, or a baked scene background. Do not use the image board itself
as a runtime background. Output is a pending candidate, never an approval decision.
