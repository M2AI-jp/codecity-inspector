# Fable5 prop source

Create one declared town prop in the single three-quarter overhead projection. Keep a clear,
functional silhouette at 32-64 pixels, a small grounded contact shadow, and a consistent lower
center pivot. Furniture used indoors must align with the shared cutaway floor; street furniture
must align with a 64x64 world tile. The prop's gameplay meaning must be recognizable without text.

No floor tile, room, street scene, people, labels, UI border, arbitrary companion objects, or
oversized concept-art rendering.
