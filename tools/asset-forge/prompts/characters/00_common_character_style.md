# Common character sprite style

Approved reference images take priority.

Characters are small town-map sprites, not large character illustrations, concept art, modern
indie oversized sprites, or character-sheet presentation art.

Fixed traits:

- small face and restrained head size
- slim, vertically oriented silhouette; no round or wide body
- role reads through hair, clothing, silhouette, and pose rather than facial detail
- controlled palette with two or three shading steps
- black generation background unless an output contract explicitly requires transparency
- logical size around 20×32 pixels and nearest-neighbor preview
- no labels, UI frame, explanatory text, or sheet decoration

Forbidden traits:

- chibi, cute, large head, round face, large anime eyes
- plush or toy-like proportions
- modern indie sprite or gacha styling
- oversized character art
- UI labels or decorative character-sheet frames
